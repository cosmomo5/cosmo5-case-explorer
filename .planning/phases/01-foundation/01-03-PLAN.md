<objective>
Wire the Phase 1 primitives into the running application: enrich cases at boot, create the ViewStateMachine and ShortlistStore at boot level, and replace hardcoded opacity in tickOrbits with the single resolveStarAppearance resolver.

Purpose: Plan 01 and Plan 02 created pure modules in isolation. This plan makes them live. The enrichment change is a zero-visible-change boot step. The ViewState wiring replaces the ad-hoc `objects.mode = 'focused'` mutations with typed dispatches while keeping backward-compat sync so all existing scene-state.ts consumers keep working unchanged. The opacity refactor in tickOrbits is the only structural scene change — it replaces `isMatch ? 0.85 : 0.15` with `resolveStarAppearance(state).opacity`.

Output: enrich.ts + test, and targeted edits to main.ts and scene-state.ts. The app must start and behave identically to before Phase 1 — no visible changes, no console errors.
</objective>
