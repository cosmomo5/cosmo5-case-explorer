## Accomplishments
- Star glow sprites: each star has a canvas-texture SpriteMaterial child at local (0,0,0) with AdditiveBlending, scale = starRadius * 7. Colored by nucleus category, opacity 0.65 (permission) or 0.2 (locked).
- Per-star twinkle: tickOrbits twinkle pass at end of function oscillates each sprite's opacity via Math.sin(t * 0.7 + phase) with per-star random phase offset. Guarded by !prefersReduced.
- Stars migrated from MeshStandardMaterial to MeshBasicMaterial — self-emissive, ignores the 3-light rig that was making them look flat.
- Reading-state gold glow: mat.color.setHex(0xe8b15a) replaces broken emissive approach (MeshBasicMaterial has no emissive). originalColor stored per star for restore.
- Nebula CSS: invisible gold (rgba 232,177,90 at 4-6%) replaced with deep blue-indigo (rgba 60,30,120 at 14% and rgba 100,50,160 at 9%) — perceptually visible on #0B0C10.

## Decisions Made
- MeshBasicMaterial for stars: the 3-light rig was treating stars as diffuse objects (lit marbles). Self-emissive material restores the "star as light source" quality from the original cosmos.js.
- Reading-state gold via color not emissive: MeshBasicMaterial has no `emissive` property. mat.color.setHex() achieves the same visual result.
- Nebula color: avoided gold (too close to the UI token 0xe8b15a) and avoided deep purple (Phase 7 will audit purple tokens). Deep blue-indigo reads as "deep space" atmospheric depth on the dark background.
